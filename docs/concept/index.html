A · The BinderyRecommended

A foil-stamped cover you can hold

Cream linen page, gold mark that catches light, products as real binders on an oak plank. The page ends in a dark hearth with embers, so the Press and the Crew share one house.

Printables look right on paper. The mark gets to be foil, as the brand doc asked. The dark ending gives the drama without making the whole site a kids' site.

B · Hearth at DuskKept as the finale

Full dark, embers everywhere

The 2026-08 Crew prototype grown up: forest green, parallax ridges, cinematic. Memorable, and half-built already.

Reads as the kids' brand. Printable previews go flat on dark. Gold on dark green is the most common luxury template online right now.

Static site, free tier, one plank of infrastructure.

Astro builds the pages. GitHub Actions ships them. An Always Free Ampere VM on your Oracle account serves them behind Caddy with automatic HTTPS. Cloudflare in front for cache and cover.
